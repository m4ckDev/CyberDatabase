## Monitoring workflow

For defensive collection, record only what is needed for threat intelligence:

- forum/community name;
- observed aliases or rebrands;
- language;
- public reporting source;
- observed threat categories;
- actor handles when relevant to an investigation;
- victim/company references;
- malware family names;
- CVEs and TTPs;
- hashes/domains/IPs suitable for detection;
- first-seen and last-seen dates;
- confidence and source reliability;
- ATT&CK mappings;
- detection or incident-response action.

Do not place purchased data, stolen credentials, personal data dumps, live malware, criminal transaction instructions, or illicit access credentials in this repository.
