## Cybersecurity OSINT workflow

For defensive and authorized cybersecurity research, a practical workflow is:

1. Preserve the original public source or page state.
2. Record the starting indicator: domain, URL, IP, hash, username, company, image, document, or other public clue.
3. Pivot through passive sources first.
4. Corroborate important findings with more than one independent source.
5. Separate infrastructure overlap from attribution claims.
6. Record timestamps, source URLs, screenshots or archived copies when evidence may change.
7. Track confidence and known limitations.
8. Convert relevant findings into defensive outputs such as IOC enrichment, ATT&CK mappings, detection ideas, exposure findings, or incident-response leads.

## Safety and privacy

Use public-source intelligence responsibly. Do not treat OSINT as permission to bypass authentication, purchase stolen data, impersonate people, access private accounts, or collect unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Some resources can expose personal, corporate, infrastructure, or breach-related information. Minimize collection to what is necessary for the legitimate investigation and verify applicable laws, terms of service, and organizational policy.

Note: A VPN may be required to access some resources because availability can vary by region, ISP, or network restrictions.
